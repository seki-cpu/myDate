## UI handoff notes

- New IDs are exactly the IDs in `src/data/dateIdeas.ts`.
- Continue using the existing localization mechanism in UI; do not duplicate separate activity arrays per language.
- Keep missions optional and visually secondary.
- Photo prompts are only used when the task is genuinely photographic.
- Do not turn missions into completion requirements, scores, or relationship tests.
- Prefer card descriptions that remain readable at mobile width without expanding the home feed excessively.
